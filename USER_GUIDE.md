# Touround CRM — User Guide

Version 1.0.3

Touround CRM is the business portal for Touround vendors. It lets you manage your
services, take and process reservations, communicate with customers, run
promotions, publish posts, track earnings, and more — all from your phone.

This guide walks through every part of the app. Use the table of contents to jump
to a section.

## 1. Getting started

Touround CRM is a mobile app for business owners and their staff. To use it you
need:

- An Android device (the app also runs on the web portal with limited charting).
- A business account on Touround. If you only have a **customer** account, use the
  Touround customer app instead — the CRM is for vendors.
- An internet connection. The app shows an offline banner when the connection
  drops and syncs again once you are back online.

> **Note:** The CRM is a separate app from the customer-facing Touround app.
> Customers browse and book through their app; you manage everything on the
> business side here.

---

## 2. Creating your account

1. On the sign-in screen, tap **Register**.
2. Fill in your business details:
   - **Business name** — at least 3 characters.
   - **Description** — a short summary of what you offer.
   - **Email** — this becomes your login and receives your verification code.
   - **Phone (+27)** — enter the local number without the leading zero
     (for example, `712345678`). The app adds the +27 country code for you.
   - **Attraction category** — pick the one that matches your business
     (for example Accommodation, Food & Beverage, Salons and Beauty). This choice
     determines how reservations work for you, so choose carefully.
   - **Location** — type your address or tap **Use my location** to set it from
     your device's GPS. You can also fine-tune the pin on the map.
   - **Password** — must contain at least 8 characters, one uppercase letter, one
     lowercase letter, one number, and one special character. A live checklist
     shows which rules you have met.
   - **Confirm password** — must match.
3. Tap **Create Business Account**.
4. **Verify your email.** Touround sends a 6-digit code to your email address.
   Enter it on the verification screen and tap **Verify Email**.
5. When you see **Email Verified**, tap **Sign In** to log in for the first time.

---

## 3. Signing in

1. Enter your **email** and **password**.
2. Tap the eye icon in the password field to show or hide what you type.
3. Tap **Sign In**.

**Forgot your password?** Tap **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in screen,
enter your email, and follow the reset instructions sent to you.

**Account deactivated?** If your account was deactivated, the app sends you to a
reactivation screen where you can restore it.

**Wrong app?** If you try to sign in with a customer account, the app tells you to
use the Touround customer app instead.

## 4. Understanding your business type

Your attraction category controls how customers reserve from you. The app adapts
its screens automatically. There are three reservation models:

| Model | Used by | What you configure |
|-------|---------|--------------------|
| **Rooms** | Accommodation | Room types, price per night, capacity, number of rooms |
| **Tables** | Food & Beverage, Entertainment & Nightlife | Table capacity, number of tables, table price, add-ons |
| **Slots** | Salons & Beauty, Games & Sports, Wellness & Fitness | Time slots with capacity, price, duration, services |

Throughout this guide, wherever you see "service," it refers to whichever model
applies to your business — rooms, tables, or slots.

## 5. Navigating the app

The app has a bottom navigation bar with five tabs:

- **Home** — your dashboard with key stats and charts.
- **Bookings** — reservations and bookings to accept, check in, and check out.
- **Posts** — your social feed where you publish updates.
- **Chat** — direct messages with customers.
- **More** — a menu that opens everything else.

Tapping **More** opens a menu grouped into three sections:

- **Business:** Services, Events & Tickets, Customers, Sales Report, Payments,
  Promotions, Staff.
- **Insights:** Reviews, Analytics.
- **Account:** Profile Settings, App Settings.

The **More** menu also has a **Sign Out** button at the bottom.

At the top of most screens you will find a search bar and a notification bell.
Tapping your profile picture opens a quick menu with Profile, Settings, and
Logout.

---

## 6. Dashboard

The **Home** tab is your at-a-glance overview. It shows:

- **Welcome card** with your business name.
- **Key stats:** Revenue, Customers, Reservations/Bookings, and Loss.
- **Revenue chart** — a bar chart of revenue over the selected period.
- **Trend chart** — a line chart of reservations/bookings over time.
- **Status breakdown** — how many of your reservations are Confirmed, Pending, or
  Cancelled.

Use the period selector (Week / Month / etc.) to change the time range. Pull down
to refresh.

> **On the web portal:** The charts are only shown in the mobile app. On the web
> you will see a short note in place of each chart; all other data still appears.

## 7. Service management

Open **More → Services** to manage what customers can book. The screen title is
"Service Management" and adapts to your business type.

### Adding a service

1. Tap the **+** button in the top-right.
2. Fill in the form (fields depend on your business type):
   - **Rooms:** room type (Single, Double, Suite, Triple, Deluxe), description,
     price per night, adult capacity, child capacity, total number of rooms.
   - **Tables:** description, capacity, number of tables, table price, add-ons,
     and a **Payment: Card or Cash** toggle.
   - **Slots:** choose the **Audience** (Adults or Kids), then add a description,
     capacity, price, duration (for example, "2 hours"), list of services,
     add-ons, and the payment toggle.
3. Add photos with **Add Images** and a clip with **Add Video** if you want.
4. Tap **Save**.

> **Slots — Adults vs Kids:** Each slot targets one audience with its own price
> and duration. To offer both, create one Adults slot and one Kids slot. On the
> card, a coloured badge shows whether a slot is for Adults or Kids.

### Editing or deleting

- Tap **Edit** on any card to change its details.
- Tap **Delete** to remove it. You will be asked to confirm.

## 8. Reservations and bookings

The **Bookings** tab lists reservation requests and active bookings. Each card
shows the reference, customer, date/time, amount, and a colour-coded status.

### Reservation lifecycle

Depending on the stage, different action buttons appear on each card:

1. **Pending / Awaiting confirmation** — tap **Accept** to confirm, or **Reject**
   to decline. Rejecting asks you for a reason, and the payment status is marked
   as failed automatically.
2. **Accepted / Confirmed** — tap **Check In** when the customer arrives, or
   **Reschedule** to change the date and time.
3. **Checked in** — tap **Check Out** when they leave. If the payment was not yet
   marked complete, checking out records it as collected on-site.

### Rescheduling

Tap **Reschedule**, pick a new date, and enter a new time, then tap **Update**.
If the new slot is unavailable, the app tells you so you can try another time.

### Viewing details

Tap a reservation to open its detail view with the full customer and booking
information. Use the search box to find a reservation by its reference.

## 9. Events and tickets

Open **More → Events & Tickets** to run ticketed events. The screen has tabs for
managing **Events** and a **Scan & Verify** tool.

### Creating an event

1. On the Events tab, tap **+**.
2. Enter the event name, date, time, location, and description.
3. Add **ticket categories** (for example GENERAL, VIP, VVIP, EARLY_BIRD,
   STUDENT, KIDS, VOUCHER). For each category set the price and the number of
   tickets available.
4. Add images and a video if you like.
5. Save the event.

Each event card shows the date, location, category, and how many tickets have
been sold. Use **Edit** and **Delete** to manage events.

### Scanning tickets at the door

1. Switch to the **Scan & Verify** view.
2. Tap **Scan QR Code** and point the camera at a customer's ticket.
3. The app shows a green check for a valid ticket or a red cross for an invalid
   one, along with the ticket details.

## 10. Posts

The **Posts** tab is your business feed, similar to a social timeline. Customers
see your posts in their app.

### Creating a post

1. In the "What's on your mind?" box, type your message (optional).
2. Tap **Photo** to attach images or **Video** to attach a clip.
3. Tap **Post**.

You can publish a post with just a photo or video — text is optional. Posts with
a single image show it full-width; posts with several images scroll sideways.

### Managing posts

- Each post shows its reaction count and comment count.
- Tap the **•••** menu on a post to delete it.
- Pull down to refresh the feed.

---

## 11. Messages

The **Chat** tab holds your conversations with customers.

### Starting a new conversation

1. Tap the **+** button.
2. In the **Find a Customer** sheet, search by the customer's email or phone
   number and tap the search icon.
3. When the customer appears, tap their card (or **Message …**) to open the chat.

### Chatting

- Type in the message box at the bottom and tap the send button.
- The message box stays visible above the keyboard so you can always see what you
  are typing.
- Incoming messages are marked as read when you open the conversation, which
  clears the unread badge.

## 12. Notifications

Open the bell icon (top of most screens) or **More** to reach notifications.

- Tap a notification to open its detail view.
- Message notifications take you straight to the related chat.
- New activity can play a sound/local notification so you do not miss it.

## 13. Customers

Open **More → Customers** to see everyone who has interacted with your business.

- Browse the customer list and tap a customer to view their details.
- Use this to look up contact information and history before responding to a
  request or message.

## 14. Payments and earnings

Open **More → Payments**. This screen has an earnings overview and a transactions
list.

### Earnings breakdown

- **Gross Sales** — total value of your sales.
- **Platform Fee (8%)** and **Service Fee (5%)** — deductions.
- **Net Earnings** — what you keep (an 87% effective rate).
- **Available balance** and **Request Payout** — withdraw once you reach the
  minimum payout threshold. The app shows how much more you need if you are below
  it.

### Commission on cash payments

If you accept cash, a **Commission Owed to Touround** banner shows the 13%
commission due on those cash payments. Tap **Settle Commission** to clear it.

### Recording a payment

1. Tap to record a payment for a reservation.
2. Choose **Record Cash Payment** (adds 13% commission to your owed balance), or
   **Send Online Payment Link (Ozow)** to send the customer a secure online
   payment link you can share.

### Transactions

Switch to the transactions view to see all payments, filtered by status.

## 15. Sales report

Open **More → Sales Report** for a detailed breakdown of your sales.

- Use the filter buttons to change the view.
- Each card shows the customer's details and the sale information.
- Tap the document icon in the header to **export** the report as a PDF you can

## 16. Promotions

Open **More → Promotions** to create special offers.

1. Tap **+** / **Create Promotion**.
2. Enter the promotion details, discount, promo code (optional), and the start and
   end dates.
3. Save it.

Each promotion card shows its discount, date range, and promo code. Edit or delete
promotions as needed.

## 17. Reviews

Open **More → Reviews** to read customer feedback.

- The top card shows your **average rating** and total number of reviews.
- Each review shows the customer, their star rating, and comment.
- Tap **Reply** to respond to a review, or **Edit reply** to change a response you
  already posted.

---

## 18. Analytics

Open **More → Analytics** for deeper insight into performance, including metrics
such as your average rating and status breakdowns. Pull down to refresh.

## 19. Staff management

Open **More → Staff** (available to the business owner) to add team members and
control what they can access.

### Roles and permissions

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| **Owner** | Everything, including staff and business settings |
| **Manager** | Everything except staff management and business settings |
| **Receptionist** | Reservations, check-in/out, customers, messages |
| **Finance** | Payments, sales reports, analytics |
| **Marketing** | Posts, promotions, reviews |
| **Events Coordinator** | Event tickets, reservations, customers |

### Adding a staff member

1. Tap to add a staff member.
2. Enter their details and choose a role.
3. Save. They can then sign in with their own credentials and will only see the
   areas their role allows.

> Only the business owner can manage staff. Other roles see a locked message on
> this screen.

## 20. Profile settings

Open **More → Profile Settings** (or the profile menu at the top of the screen) to
manage your business profile.

- Update your **cover photo** and **logo/avatar** by tapping the camera badge on
  each.
- Edit your business name, description, contact details, and location (with the
  same map/GPS picker used at registration).
- Save your changes.

## 21. App settings

Open **More → App Settings** to personalise the app.

- **Theme colour** — choose an accent colour (Blue, Purple, Green, Red, Orange,
  Teal, Pink, Indigo). Icons and highlights across the app update to match.
- **Light / Dark mode** — switch between a bright interface and a darker one.

Your choices are saved on the device.

## 22. Troubleshooting and FAQ

**I can't sign in — it says my account is a customer account.**
The CRM is only for business accounts. Use the Touround customer app with that
login, or register a separate business account.

**My account is deactivated.**
Sign in and follow the reactivation prompt to restore it.

**I didn't get my verification code.**
Check your spam folder. You can go back and try again, or re-register with the
same email.

**A reservation won't reschedule.**
The time slot you chose may be unavailable. Pick a different date or time.

**I added a slot for Kids but it still shows Adults.**
Each slot is for a single audience. Make sure the **Audience** toggle is set to
**Kids** before you save, and create a separate slot for each audience.

**My post image looks blank.**
Make sure the upload finished before posting. If an image fails to load it is
hidden automatically so you will not see an empty box.

**The dashboard charts are missing on the web portal.**
Charts are shown in the mobile app only. All underlying numbers still appear on
the web.

**I'm offline.**
The app shows an offline banner and reconnects automatically. Changes you make
while offline sync when the connection returns.

**How are my earnings calculated?**
Touround deducts an 8% platform fee and a 5% service fee, leaving you 87% net.
Cash payments add a 13% commission that you settle from the Payments screen.
